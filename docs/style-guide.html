Style guide

Direction: high fashion, chic, sleek, muted. Couture in feel, functional in use. Quiet warm neutrals, thin lines, lots of space, an editorial serif, and small tracked capitals. The ten tone swatches are the only strong color on the page, so people’s skin tones are the stars.

Color

Interface colors are muted warm neutrals. Contrast ratios are measured against Bone, and body text needs 4.5:1 or better.

Accessibility rules

  • Body text is at least 4.5:1 against its background. Taupe and Hairline are decoration only.
  • Every swatch has a visible number and name, and selection never relies on color alone.
  • Every control has a visible focus ring (2px ink, 3px offset).
  • Swatches are real radio buttons in a fieldset with a legend, so keyboard and screen readers work.
  • Motion is limited to small hover transitions, and is turned off for people who ask their device for less motion.
