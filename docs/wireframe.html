  • ATitle and short intro. Says what to do: pick the closest swatch. A small note explains lighting and that a selfie estimate comes later.
  • BTen tone swatches, 01 to 10. Each is a square with its number, name and hex code. They are radio buttons, so the keyboard arrow keys move between them.
  • CSelection text. Says “Selected: Shade 06” in words. The chosen square also shows a thick frame and a “Selected” label, so selection never depends on color alone. “Clear selection” undoes it.
  • DSee my looks. The only main action. It goes to Screen 2 with the chosen level in the address (?tone=6).

On a phone the intro stacks above the swatches, and the swatches become two columns of squares. The button spans the full width.

  • EBack link. “Change tone” returns to Screen 1.
  • FChosen tone. The swatch, its name and hex, with a “Change” link. Read from the API, not typed in.
  • GCategory tabs. Foundation works first. The others are visible but disabled with “soon”, so people see where the project is going without clicking into nothing.
  • HUnreviewed toggle. Off by default, so people only see reviewed matches. Turn it on to see proposals, which are clearly labeled unverified.
  • IResult cards. Each shows brand, product, shade name, the brand’s own wording, our match confidence, a review badge, and Product and Source links. The top strip shows the shade color only when a source gave one.
